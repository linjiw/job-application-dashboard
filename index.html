What the feedback actually tells us

How to interpret these numbers

Observed rejection share is rejections divided by all submitted roles in the selected cohort. Pending applications stay in the denominator. Interview reach counts roles that have ever reached an interview; it is not an offer rate.

Small samples, different submission dates, company requirements and hiring speed make categories hard to compare. No response does not mean rejection. Generic feedback does not identify an ATS, keyword, skills or eligibility cause.
